## ★ The Signature 10 (if you only build from this file, build these)

These are the highest-leverage new ideas — the ones that make AlcheMix feel like
*itself* and not "another quiz app."

1. **Emission Spectra & Flame Tests** ⭐ 🟡 🔒moat — burn an element, see its real
   flame colour + emission-line spectrum. **This is a gift for your glowing night-sky
   aesthetic** — literally the most beautiful, on-brand chemistry topic there is, and it
   teaches quantum shells/energy levels for real. Do this first.
2. **The Discovery Tree** ⭐ 🟡 🔒moat — a Little-Alchemy-style encyclopedia of every
   forge-able compound, with locked silhouettes and a "you've discovered 34%" bar. Turns
   mixing from a one-off into a *collection drive*. This is your retention engine.
3. **Conditions in the Cauldron** ⭐ 🟡 — add **heat / pressure / catalyst** sliders to
   the mixing sandbox so the *same* reactants give *different* products. Teaches Le
   Chatelier and "why reactions don't just happen" without a textbook.
4. **DepEd K-12 curriculum tagging** ⭐ 🟡 🔒moat — tag every module/quiz to the actual
   Philippine science competencies. **This is the single biggest thing that turns a cool
   demo into something a real teacher can adopt tomorrow.** Nobody international bothers.
5. **Classroom Projector Mode** ⭐ 🟢 — a big-screen, no-login "molecule/element of the
   day" + live class quiz display the teacher casts to the front of the room. Kahoot's
   whole growth came from the room, not the phone.
6. **The Great Work (class collaborative table)** ⭐ 🟡 🔒moat — the whole class's
   discoveries fill *one shared* periodic table / build one giant class molecule. Group
   goal → social pressure → engagement. Pure Firestore-live.
7. **"Failed" reactions that teach** 🟢 🔒moat — invalid combos return an in-character
   alchemist rejection ("The cauldron spurns inert gases") *plus* the real reason. Turns
   every wrong mix into a micro-lesson instead of a dead end.
8. **Limiting-Reagent Puzzle mode** 🟡 — stoichiometry as a resource game: "You have 3 H₂
   and 2 O₂ — brew the most water." Makes the driest topic in chem a puzzle.
9. **Build-a-Molecule with octet enforcement** ⭐ 🟡 — drag atoms together; the app only
   lets bonds form when valence/octet rules are satisfied. Your "mix and match" idea,
   grown up into real bonding — this is the natural evolution of your original concept.
10. **Adaptive "Alchemist's Path"** ⭐ 🔴 — an AI-generated personal storyline that
    routes each student through topics based on mastery, framed as a hero's journey
    (Apprentice → Adept → Master). Difficulty-tuning from your doc, but as *narrative*.

## 2. Chemistry depth that stays *fun* (not textbook bloat)

The trap you named — "in-depth chemistry website" — is avoided by making every topic a
*mechanic*, not a page of text.

- **Emission spectra / flame tests** ⭐ 🔒 — (Signature #1) energy levels, gorgeous.
- **Redox as "charge duels"** — electron transfer visualised as a tug-of-war. 🟡
- **pH / acid–base "potion brewing"** — titration reframed as balancing a brew to a
  target colour. (Titration is in your doc; the *potion* framing is the new part.) 🟡
- **Reverse ID mode** — show a 3D structure, student names it / the bond type. 🟢
- **Kitchen Chemistry quests** — AI-graded photo missions for *real* home reactions
  (rust, baking soda + vinegar, caramelising sugar). Extends your evidence journal into
  the student's actual life. ⭐ 🟡 🔒
- **Nucleosynthesis / "where elements come from"** — a short astro time-lapse: which
  elements are forged in stars vs supernovae. Ties the *whole periodic table* to a story
  and fits your cosmic aesthetic perfectly. 🟡 ⭐
- **Organic functional groups as "runes"** — a gentle on-ramp to organic chem. 🔴

## 7. Student-owned outcomes (the part schools actually pay for)

- **Mastery Transcript / "Grimoire of Achievements"** ⭐ — an exportable record of every
  concept mastered and card discovered; a portfolio a student (or parent, or next

  calibrated confidence. Cheap, and genuinely better pedagogy. 🟢

## How to choose from this (tie back to the spine)

From `my_thoughts.md`, the recommendation was **platform-first, game as the protected
hook.** Under that spine, the ranking of *this* file is roughly:

1. **Signature #1 (spectra), #2 (discovery tree), #7 (failed-reactions), #9 (build-a-
   molecule)** — deepen the *one vertical slice* and protect the hook.
2. **Signature #4 (DepEd tags), #5 (projector mode)** + §5 teacher tools — what makes it
   *adoptable* by a real class (the platform half of the spine).
3. **Signature #6 (Great Work), §3 Houses/Duels** — engagement, once the core loop is
   solid.
4. Everything else = flavour to add *after* one full loop is excellent — not before.

**Rule of thumb:** if an idea here doesn't make *the single loop* (scan → build → react →
feedback → teacher sees it) better, it waits. Breadth is the enemy now; depth on the hook
is the win.
