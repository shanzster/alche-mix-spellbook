What is AlcheMix?

AlcheMix is a chemistry learning platform wearing the cloak of alchemy. Beneath the robes, every number is textbook-true — real bond types, real reactions, real elements — taught through a world of grimoires, trials, and forging.

It begins with a deck of 12 physical trading cards, the base elements. Point a phone at a card and it wakes in augmented reality; carry the deck to the website and the full Grimoire opens.

Chapter III — The Astrolabe

Why Schools Should Implement This.

The apprentices collect secrets. Their educators hold the astrolabe — and see everything.

  1. I.

    A console built for teaching

    Classes with join codes, a quiz builder seeded from the curriculum, mission configurator, and a gradebook — authored once, delivered to every apprentice.

  2. II.

    Sight before the exam

    A live performance matrix surfaces struggling concepts per student and per class — weeks before a test would have revealed them.

  3. III.

    Real chemistry, honest motivation

    Every value is textbook-accurate, and practice counters are engagement — never grades. Marks stay where they belong: with the teacher.

  4. IV.

    Runs on what you already have

    Any phone camera wakes the AR cards; the site works offline, and every AI feature has a no-key fallback. No lab, no licenses, no special hardware.

  5. V.

    A tangible thing to own

    Twelve physical cards per apprentice — something to hold, trade, and keep. The deck makes the chemistry classroom's quietest subject collectible.
