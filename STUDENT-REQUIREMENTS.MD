# Alchemix: Student Interactive Features

This document outlines the core interactive modules and activities exclusively available to student users within the Alchemix platform.

## 1. Multimodal AI AR Scavenger Hunt
* **Webcam Marker Tracking:** Uses the client-side camera matrix framework to detect printed **Element Sigil Cards** from *The Alchemist's Grimoire* and overlay interactive 3D visual models on the live video stream.
* **AI-Assisted Mission Submission:** Captures base64 visual frames and handwritten solution entries, routing them to the multimodal AI endpoint for instant conceptual verification.
* **Real-World Lab Scenarios:** Presents curriculum-anchored problem-solving quests (e.g., balancing real-world combustion equations or diagnosing chemical safety hazards).

## 2. Virtual Chemistry Simulation Engine
* **Interactive Subatomic Particles & Atomic Builder:** Allows students to manually adjust Protons, Neutrons, and Electrons to construct stable atoms, visualize electron shell capacities (Bohr Model), and identify **Isotopes** and **Ions** dynamically.
* **Rule-Based Valency & Molecular Bonding Workspace:** Interactive workspace where students combine elements to form valid compounds, governed by valence rules and structural geometries.
* **Stoichiometry & Interactive Equation Balancer:** Displays 3D representations of chemical reactions where students manipulate coefficients to balance unbalanced chemical equations with real-time visual feedback.
* **Gas Laws & Reaction Rate Simulator:** Interactive closed-container simulation environment where students manipulate sliders for **Temperature, Volume, and Pressure** to observe Kinetic Molecular Theory behaviors in real-time.

## 3. Interactive 3D Periodic Table & Spatial Viewport
* **Interactive Element Exploration:** Allows 3D spatial rotation, zoom, and inspection of element cards containing atomic weight, electron configuration, electronegativity, and physical states.
* **Property Filtering:** Filters elements based on periodic trends (alkali metals, halogens, noble gases, block structures).

## 4. Domain-Specific Chemistry Assessments & Quizzes
* **3D Visual Quizzes:** Instead of standard text-based multiple-choice questions, students identify elements, valency bonds, and molecular structures directly by interacting with 3D models.
* **Time-Attack Equation Balancing:** A gamified assessment mode where students race against a timer to correctly balance a series of chemical equations.
* **AI-Generated Problem Sets:** Dynamically generated chemistry word problems (e.g., Gas Law calculations) ensuring each student receives a unique set of variables to prevent academic dishonesty.
* **Automated Grading & Feedback:** Instantaneous evaluation of quiz submissions with step-by-step mathematical breakdowns provided for incorrect stoichiometry or valency answers.

## 5. Gamification & Student Progress Tracker
* **Exp & Mastery Badges:** Logs completed modules, accurate balancing quests, and AR scavenger hunts to award digital points and achievement badges.
* **Personal Learning Analytics:** Displays a student-facing dashboard showing completed topics, accuracy rates per module, and areas needing review.
